How it works

  1. Fill in your company details below.
  2. On this device, that is all: the trackers offer Use saved company profile, and the Deployment Maker uses it by itself.
  3. For another device or a colleague, click Save profile to download a Company-….json file, then click Import company details in a tool and choose it. Name, contact number, address, CQC registration and logo all come across.

Company details

Printed at the top of everything the tools produce, and in the “if found” notice on bag labels. Kept on this device until you clear it.

Tick if your organisation is registered with the Care Quality Commission. Both trackers print the number beside the company name.
