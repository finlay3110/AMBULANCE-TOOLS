Back up everything

Saved work lives only in this browser. Download one file with the saved work of every tool — company profile, bags, registers, matrices, deployments and vehicle checks — to keep it safe, or to move it to another device or browser. On an iPhone or iPad, the home-screen app keeps its own saved work, separate from Safari: back up in one and restore in the other to move it across.

Everything runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded. Once opened, the tools work offline, and you can add them to your phone’s home screen like an app. All the tools are provided as is, with no warranty: read the disclaimer before use.
