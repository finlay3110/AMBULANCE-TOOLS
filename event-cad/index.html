Staffing

Each unit and its callsign, with the staff on it. Ambulances can take patients to hospital. Starts at is the standby point the unit is at when the event begins.

Locations

Points of interest, standby points and treatment centres (add as many as the event has), each with its site map grid reference and what3words address if you have them: they fill in on a call when you pick the location. They are the choices for a call’s location, where a unit takes a patient, and where it goes when it clears.

Hospitals

The hospitals ambulances may take patients to.

Quick log phrases

Updates you log often, one per line. Each becomes a one-tap button on every call’s log.

Alerts, targets and what3words

A call that passes its category’s target with no unit on scene turns amber, and the report shows how many calls met their target.

Response time targets: minutes from the call to the first unit on scene (blank for none)

With a key, the CAD suggests real what3words addresses as you type one, to catch misheard words. A free key from what3words’ developer site is enough. The key stays on this device: it is not in any export or backup. Without a key, addresses are still recorded, with a link to see them on the map.

Site map

A picture of the event’s site map (JPEG or PNG). It shows on the CAD tab, to read grid references and locations, and prints in the paper fallback pack.

With the grid set, you can click the map to fill in a call’s grid reference, and the report shows the calls on the map. Letters run across from the left, numbers down from the top.

Paper fallback pack

Print this before the event, in case the laptop fails: a unit status sheet with your callsigns, a call list, a blank call sheet and METHANE form to copy, and your locations, hospitals and the MPDS protocols.

Save the setup

Save the company, event, staffing, locations and alerts as a file, to reuse or to set up another device. Importing a setup replaces this one, before any calls are logged.

Control log

Anything that is not a call, such as “Gates open” or “Spoke to police silver”. Each entry is timestamped and printed in the report.

METHANE message

For a major incident, declared or on standby. Saved messages go in the control log and the report, and each prints as its own PDF.

Attendance and weather

Each hour, how many people are on site (from the gates) and the weather. The report shows the patients seen in each hour and the rate per 1,000 people there.

Lost and vulnerable persons

Lost children and vulnerable adults, apart from the medical calls. While someone is missing they stay in the alerts; record where they were found and who they were handed to.

Major incident casualty count

Record the casualties by triage category as the numbers come in. Each count is timestamped, goes in the control log and the report, and fills in the “N” of the next METHANE message.

End-of-event report

An A4 PDF with the calls and response times, outcomes and conveyances, NHS ambulance requests, unit workload, the treatment centre counts, the control log and any METHANE messages. Duplicate calls are left out of the figures.

The spreadsheet is a .csv file with one row per call, for Excel or Google Sheets: to compare events, or to send figures on.

Live backup file

Choose a file once, on this laptop or a USB stick, and the CAD saves a copy of everything into it after every change. If this browser’s data is lost, open the file with Import CAD on any laptop to carry on. After the browser restarts, press Reconnect to start it again.

Save the whole CAD

Everything is kept in this browser only. Export the CAD to keep it, or to carry on from another laptop: import the file there. Back up everything on the home page includes it too.

Compare with another event

Choose the CAD file from another event — last year’s, say — and the report shows the main figures side by side. Only the figures are kept, not that event’s calls.

Finalise the event

When the event is over, finalise it: the CAD is locked so the calls, logs and counts can’t change, and the report is marked final. Unlocking asks first and goes in the control log.

Start a new event

Clears the calls, counts, control log, METHANE messages, casualty counts, hospital statuses and breaks, and unlocks the CAD. The company, staffing, locations and alerts are kept. Export the CAD first if you want to keep this event.
