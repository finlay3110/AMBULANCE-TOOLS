1. What it does

Deployment Maker plans who is deployed where at an event, and produces a printed deployment log for the day. For each unit it records the callsign, whether it is an ambulance (with its registration), where it is posted or whether it roams, its kit bag and drug bag, and the people on it: name, clinical grade, radio and whether they are a manager.

On the day, event control can sign people in, record breaks and stand people down in the app, or print the log and do it on paper. Either way, signatures are done on paper.

Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no upload. Once opened, it works offline.

2. Getting started

Open the Ambulance Tools home page and choose Deployment Maker. Set up your Company Profile first (Step 1 on the home page): the Deployment Maker picks it up by itself.

  1. Event — start from a plan, check the company, fill in the event.
  2. Units — each unit and the people on it.
  3. On the day — sign people in, breaks, stand down.
  4. Generate — the PDF, and saving for later.

3. The event

4. Units and people

Type a callsign and press Add unit. Then, on the unit:

  • Roaming, or the location it is posted to.
  • Ambulance — tick it and it asks for the vehicle registration.
  • Kit bag and Drug bag — untick them for a unit with none, such as event control. Leave a number blank to write it in on the day. Bag numbers set up in the Bag Tracker and Drug Bag Tracker on this device are suggested as you type.
  • People — name, clinical grade, radio and a Manager tick. Leave a name blank for a post still to fill. + Add person adds a row.
  • Copy makes the next callsign (Alpha 1 to Alpha 2) with the same set-up and nobody on it yet. ↑ ↓ reorder; Remove deletes the unit.

5. On the day

  • Tap Sign in, Start break, End break or Stand down: each records the time now. Someone can stand down without taking a break.
  • Type over a time to change it, or clear it to undo.
  • A post with no name has a name box, to fill in as the person arrives.
  • Clear all times starts the day again, keeping names and units.

The times are filled in on the deployment log. Signatures are still collected on paper.

6. The PDF

A landscape A4 deployment log. Each unit is its own block: a dark band with the callsign (tagged AMBULANCE or ROAMING), a strip with its location, registration and bags, and a row per person with boxes for signed in, break started, break ended, stood down and a signature. Managers have a green MANAGER badge. Anything left blank is a box to write in.

With Add a radio list page ticked, a radio list for event control follows: every person with their unit, location, grade and radio.

7. Plans for venues

A venue that uses the same units each time needs a plan: the units, callsigns, locations, ambulances, which units have bags, and each post’s grade and manager tick, with the names, radios, bag numbers, registrations, times and date left blank.

  • Save as plan keeps it on this device, named after the venue, listed on the Event tab with Open and Delete.
  • Download plan (.json) saves it as a file, to share or open on another device with Open a plan or deployment file.
  • Open the plan, fill in the names on screen, or print it and fill them in by hand.

8. Saving and backing up

Everything is saved in this browser as you type. Save deployment (.json) keeps a whole deployment, times included, as a file. Back up everything on the home page saves every tool’s work, plans included, in one file, and reminds you when a backup is due.

9. Phones, tablets and offline

The tool works on a phone or tablet, and the On the day buttons are big enough to tap.

Once opened with a connection, it works offline, and can be added to the home screen like an app: in Chrome, Install app; on an iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen. On an iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own saved work, separate from Safari, so use Back up everything to move it across.

10. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
“Add at least one unit”Add a unit on the Units tab first.
“Unit 2 has no callsign”Give every unit a callsign.
No company at the topCreate a Company Profile on the home page, or use Import company details.
My deployment has goneBrowser storage was cleared, or this is a different browser. Restore your last backup on the home page, or open your saved .json.
“Not a plan or deployment saved from this tool”The file is from another tool. Plans and deployments are .json files saved from the Deployment Maker.
