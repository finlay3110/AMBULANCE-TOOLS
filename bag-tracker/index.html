Bag Tracker

Kit list, checklist and sign in/out log for a medical bag

Start from a saved file

Load a bag you exported earlier — the quickest way to set up another bag of the same layout.

Company details

Printed at the top of every sheet. Made a company profile? Use it, or import its file, to fill these in, logo included.

Create a company profile

Tick if your organisation is registered with the Care Quality Commission. The registration number is printed beside the company name.
Company logo
PNG or JPEG, printed at the top of each sheet.

Bag details

The version and date are printed on every page, so crews can tell when a printed checklist is out of date.

Modules and pouches

Add each module or pouch in the bag, then list what should be inside it and the minimum quantity of each. Items left loose in the bag can go in a pouch called “Main compartment”.

No pouches yet. Add the first one above.

Generate paperwork

One A4 PDF with the sheets ticked below, for each bag number.

QR stickers for this bag

Stick one on the bag. Scanning it with a phone camera opens this tool with the bag’s pouches and kit list loaded, ready to check. One sheet per bag number. Make new stickers if you change the pouches.

Save / load bag

Everything stays on this device — nothing is uploaded. Browser storage can be cleared, so export the bag to keep it.

Check this bag

Count each item: type the number found, or tap ✓ when all are there. Anything under its minimum shows as short. Then sign below and download the completed checklist.

Checked by

Everyone who checked the bag — say both of a crew at the start of a shift — adds their name and clinical grade and signs with a finger or the mouse. The time is recorded.

Provided as is, with no warranty. This tool formats what you type — it does not check it. You are responsible for verifying the contents of every bag.
